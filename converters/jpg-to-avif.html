    When to Use JPG to AVIF Conversion?

    Use this converter when you want to modernize your image library for the web. AVIF is supported by all major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and provides superior compression algorithms compared to the aging JPEG standard. It is ideal for:

    • E-commerce sites: Faster product page loads lead to higher conversion rates.
    • Photography portfolios: Display high-quality images with smaller bandwidth usage.
    • Blogging: Improve Core Web Vitals scores for better Google rankings.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why should I convert JPG to AVIF?
    AVIF offers significantly better compression than JPG, often reducing file sizes by 40-50% while maintaining identical visual quality. This improves page load speeds and Core Web Vitals scores.
    Is this JPG to AVIF converter safe?
    Yes, 100%. All conversions happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy.
    Can I convert multiple JPG files at once?
    Yes, you can upload and convert up to 15 JPG images simultaneously. The tool will process them in batch and allow you to download them individually or as a ZIP.
    Does AVIF support transparency?
    Yes, unlike standard JPG, AVIF supports alpha transparency, making it a superior replacement for PNG in many use cases as well.
