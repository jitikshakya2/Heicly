Converting AVIF to JPG on Windows, Mac, Linux and Mobile

A few notes if you're troubleshooting AVIF files on a specific device, outside of using a web converter:

On Windows

Windows 10 and 11 can display AVIF images with the HEIF Image Extensions installed from the Microsoft Store, but many apps still don't support it natively. Browser-based conversion bypasses this limitation entirely.

On Mac

macOS Monterey and later include native AVIF support in Preview and Safari, but older versions require third-party tools or online converters.

On Linux

Most Linux distributions need additional packages (like libavif) to handle AVIF files. A browser converter works without any system-level installation.

On Mobile

iOS 16+ and Android 12+ have added AVIF support, but many social media apps and messaging platforms still prefer JPG for compatibility.

Why convert AVIF to JPG specifically?

  • Universal compatibility — JPG opens on virtually every device, OS, browser, and app ever made.
  • Social media ready — all major platforms accept JPG without re-compression issues.
  • Email friendly — JPG attachments work seamlessly across all email clients.
  • Print support — photo labs and printing services universally accept JPG files.
