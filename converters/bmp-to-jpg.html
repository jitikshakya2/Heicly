    When to Use BMP to JPG Conversion?

    BMP is an uncompressed format that produces very large files. Convert to JPG when you need to:

    • Share via email: BMP files are often too large for email attachments.
    • Upload to websites: JPG loads much faster, improving user experience and SEO.
    • Save storage space: Reduce disk usage by 80-90% without noticeable quality loss.
    • Create backups: Keep archival copies in a more practical format.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why convert BMP to JPG?
    BMP files are uncompressed and extremely large. Converting to JPG reduces file sizes by 80-90% while maintaining good visual quality, making them practical for web use and sharing.
    Will I lose quality converting BMP to JPG?
    JPG uses lossy compression, so there is some quality loss. However, at high quality settings, the difference is barely noticeable while achieving dramatic file size reductions.
    Is this BMP converter safe?
    Yes, 100%. All conversions happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your BMP files are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy.
    How many BMP files can I convert at once?
