    When to Use PNG to AVIF Conversion?

    AVIF is an excellent replacement for PNG, especially when you need transparency but want better compression. Use this converter for:

    • Website logos and icons: Smaller file sizes mean faster page loads.
    • Screenshots with transparency: Preserve alpha channels while reducing storage.
    • Mobile apps: Reduce app bundle sizes by using AVIF assets instead of PNG.
    • E-commerce product images: Faster loading product galleries improve user experience.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does PNG to AVIF conversion preserve transparency?
    Yes! AVIF fully supports alpha transparency just like PNG. You can convert transparent PNGs to AVIF without losing the transparent background.
    Is AVIF smaller than PNG?
    Generally, yes. AVIF uses modern compression algorithms that can reduce file sizes by 50-70% compared to PNG while maintaining similar visual quality, especially for photographs and complex images.
    Is this converter safe for sensitive images?
    Absolutely. All conversions happen entirely in your browser. Your PNG files never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.
    How many PNG files can I convert at once?
    You can convert up to 15 PNG files simultaneously. The tool processes them in batch and provides individual downloads plus a ZIP option.
