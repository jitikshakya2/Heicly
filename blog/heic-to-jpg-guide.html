Convert HEIC to JPG: Free Online Guide

By Heicly Team · Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

If you've ever airdropped or emailed a photo from an iPhone and had it show up as a file nobody can open, you've run into HEIC. It's Apple's default photo format since iOS 11 — smaller and more efficient than JPG, but poorly supported outside Apple's own ecosystem. Converting HEIC to JPG fixes that instantly, and it's the single most common conversion people search for.

This guide covers how to convert HEIC to JPG for free, directly in your browser, with no software to install and no account to create.

Converting HEIC to JPG on iPhone, Mac, Windows and Android

A few notes if you're troubleshooting HEIC files on a specific device, outside of using a web converter:

On iPhone

You can set your iPhone to save new photos as JPG instead of HEIC by going to Settings → Camera → Formats and choosing "Most Compatible." This only affects photos taken after the change — existing HEIC files still need converting.

On Mac

macOS Preview can open and export HEIC files to JPG one at a time via File → Export, though it doesn't offer batch conversion the way a dedicated tool does.

On Windows

Windows doesn't open HEIC files natively without installing an extra codec pack from the Microsoft Store — which is exactly the friction a browser-based converter avoids entirely.

On Android

Most Android devices can't preview HEIC files at all by default, since it's an Apple-originated format — a browser converter is usually the fastest fix here.

Why convert HEIC to JPG specifically?

  • Universal compatibility — JPG opens on virtually every device, OS, browser, and app ever made.
  • Smaller file sizes for sharing — JPG's compression makes it ideal for email attachments, messaging apps, and web uploads with size limits.
  • Editor support — every photo editor, from Photoshop to free mobile apps, handles JPG without needing plugins.
  • Printing — most print labs and photo kiosks expect JPG and may reject HEIC outright.
