Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Heicly ("we", "this site") provides a free, in-browser HEIC image converter. This policy explains what happens to your files, and separately, how advertising on this site works.

Image conversion runs entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. Your HEIC files are never uploaded, transmitted to a server, or stored anywhere. We have no access to the images you convert, and no record of them is kept.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on someone's prior visits to this website or other websites. These cookies are set by the ad provider, not by Heicly, and are unrelated to the images you convert.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand approximate traffic and page performance (e.g. how many people visit, which pages are popular). This data is aggregated and is not linked to the files you convert.

Information we do not collect
