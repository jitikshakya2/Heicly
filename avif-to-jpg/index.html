    Formats involved
    A AVIF AV1 Image File Format

    A modern image format based on the AV1 video codec. Delivers exceptional compression with high quality — smaller than JPEG and WebP at equivalent visual fidelity. Supported by newer browsers and apps.

    O JPG Joint Photographic Experts Group

    The most universally supported image format on Earth. Every browser, operating system, email client, and social platform accepts JPG without question. Ideal for sharing and archiving.

    Why convert AVIF files?

    AVIF offers superior compression, but compatibility is still catching up. Converting to JPG ensures your images work everywhere.

    Email clients frequently struggle with AVIF attachments. JPG ensures your recipients can actually view the photos you send.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Is this actually free?
    Yes — there's no server cost to cover per conversion since your own browser does the work, so there's no metered usage to charge for.
    Does it work on mobile?
    Yes. Any modern mobile browser can run the conversion — tap the dropzone to open your photo library, just like uploading anywhere else.
    What quality should I choose?
    The default 92% is ideal for most photos — excellent quality with reasonable file size. Use 100% for archival or editing, and lower values (70–85%) for web use where smaller files matter more.
    Will this work on very large files?
    Most photos convert in a second or two. Very large files (30MB+) or unusual AVIF variants can take longer or occasionally fail — that's a limitation of in-browser decoding, not a bug.
