What is an AVIF File?

What is an AVIF file, exactly? AVIF stands for AV1 Image File Format, a modern image format developed by the Alliance for Open Media. Based on the AV1 video codec, AVIF represents a significant leap forward in image compression technology.

Unlike traditional formats like JPEG that date back to 1992, AVIF leverages advanced encoding techniques to deliver:

  • Superior compression efficiency — AVIF files are typically 50% smaller than equivalent quality JPG files
  • Better visual quality — Reduced artifacts, smoother gradients, and better detail preservation
  • HDR support — High Dynamic Range imaging for richer colors and contrast
  • Wide color gamut — Support for modern color spaces like Rec. 2020
  • Transparency — Alpha channel support similar to PNG
  • Animation — Support for animated images like GIF but with better compression

Major tech companies including Google, Netflix, Amazon, and Microsoft back the AVIF format. It's increasingly used for web images, streaming content, and modern applications. However, despite these advantages, AVIF adoption remains incomplete across the digital ecosystem.

Most social media platforms, messaging apps, and content management systems expect JPG uploads. While some platforms now accept AVIF, they often re-encode images anyway, potentially introducing quality loss. Uploading JPG directly gives you control over the final quality.

Editing Workflow

While newer versions of Photoshop and other editing tools support AVIF, many plugins, filters, and workflows are optimized for JPG. Converting ensures compatibility throughout your entire editing pipeline.

Benefits of JPG Format

Despite being over 30 years old, JPEG remains the most widely used image format for good reasons:

  • Maximum compatibility — Opens on literally every device and platform
  • Small file sizes — Efficient lossy compression suitable for photos
  • Adjustable quality — Control the balance between file size and image quality

  • Mature ecosystem — Decades of optimization and tooling support
  • Color profile support — Works with sRGB, Adobe RGB, and other color spaces
