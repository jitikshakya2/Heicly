    When to Use HEIC to PDF Conversion?

    HEIC is efficient for storage on iPhones, but PDF is the global standard for document exchange. Use this converter when:

    • Submitting documents: Visa applications, government forms, and school submissions often require PDF.
    • Creating photo albums: Combine multiple photos into a single, easy-to-share PDF booklet.
    • Printing: Print shops prefer PDF files for consistent formatting and quality.
    • Sharing with non-Apple users: Ensure anyone can view your photos without needing special software.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do I need to convert HEIC to PDF?
    HEIC is Apple's proprietary format that isn't universally supported. PDF is a universal standard accepted by governments, schools, businesses, and printing services worldwide.
    Can I merge multiple HEIC photos into one PDF?
    Yes! You can upload up to 15 HEIC images, and they will be combined into a single PDF document with each photo on a separate page.
    Is my data safe when converting HEIC to PDF?
    100% safe. All conversions happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy.
    Does this work on Windows and Android?
    Yes! Even though HEIC is an Apple format, our converter works on any device with a modern web browser, including Windows PCs, Android phones, and Linux computers.
