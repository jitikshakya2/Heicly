    When to Use TIFF to JPG Conversion?

    TIFF is a lossless format preferred by photographers and printers, but it creates very large files. Convert to JPG when you need to:

    • Share photos online: JPG is universally supported by social media, websites, and messaging apps.
    • Send via email: Reduce file sizes to stay within email attachment limits.
    • Upload to websites: Faster page loads improve user experience and SEO rankings.
    • Create backups: Save storage space while keeping visually identical copies.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why convert TIFF to JPG?
    TIFF files are large and ideal for printing, but JPG is better for web use, email, and sharing. Converting to JPG reduces file size by up to 90% while maintaining good visual quality.
    Will I lose quality converting TIFF to JPG?
    JPG uses lossy compression, so there is some quality loss. However, at high quality settings (which we use), the difference is barely noticeable to the human eye while achieving significant file size reduction.
    Is this TIFF to JPG converter safe?
    Yes, 100%. All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your TIFF files are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.
    How many TIFF files can I convert at once?
    You can convert up to 15 TIFF files simultaneously. The tool processes them in batch and provides individual downloads plus a ZIP option for convenience.
