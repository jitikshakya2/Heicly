    When to Use AVIF to PNG Conversion

    AVIF (AV1 Image File Format) is a modern, highly efficient image format that provides excellent compression and quality. However, PNG remains the most universally compatible image format. Convert AVIF to PNG when you need to:

    • Share photos on social media — Most social platforms still prefer or require PNG format for optimal display.
    • Email images — PNG ensures recipients can view your photos regardless of their device or email client.
    • Upload to websites — Many content management systems and older websites don't yet support AVIF.
    • Print photos — Professional printing services typically require PNG or TIFF formats.
    • Ensure maximum compatibility — PNG works on every device, browser, and application without exception.

    Our AVIF to PNG converter processes files entirely in your browser using the Canvas API, ensuring your photos remain private while delivering fast, high-quality conversions.
